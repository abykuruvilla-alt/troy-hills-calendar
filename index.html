To subscribe and receive future calendar updates:

  1. Open Google Calendar on a computer.
  2. Next to Other calendars, click +.
  3. Select From URL.
  4. Copy the calendar address below.
  5. Paste it into Google Calendar.
  6. Click Add calendar.
